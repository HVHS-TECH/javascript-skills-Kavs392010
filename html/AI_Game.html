🎮 ANGRY BIRDS GAME 🎮

About This Game:

Launch colorful birds at enemy pigs and wooden structures to destroy them all! Progress through 4 challenging levels with increasing difficulty. Each level is harder than the last with more enemies and structures. Your score carries over across all levels!

How to Play:

  • Click and drag on the launcher (left side) to aim the bird
  • Release to launch the bird at enemies
  • Different birds have different speeds, damages, and abilities
  • Destroy all green pigs and wooden structures to complete each level
  • You get a certain number of birds per level
  • Press SPACE to advance to the next level after completing one

Levels:

  • Level 1: 2 enemies, 3 structures, 5 birds available
  • Level 2: 3 enemies, 4 structures, 6 birds available
  • Level 3: 4 enemies, 5 structures, 7 birds available
  • Level 4: 5 enemies, 4 structures, 8 birds available

Birds:

  • 🔴 Red - Balanced damage and speed
  • 🟡 Chuck - Fast but less powerful
  • ⚫ Bomb - Extremely powerful, slowest
  • 🔴 Terence - Most powerful, very slow
  • 🟠 Stella - Good damage and speed

Scoring:

  • Destroy an enemy pig: +100 points
  • Destroy a structure: +50 points
  • Your score persists across all levels!
  • Hits Counter: Tracks how many enemies and structures you've destroyed
