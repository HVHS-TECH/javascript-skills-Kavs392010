🚀 SPACE INVADERS 🚀

About This Game:

Defend Earth from waves of alien invaders! Shoot down enemies before they reach the bottom of the screen. Progress through 5 levels of increasing difficulty with new enemy formations and more aggressive aliens!

How to Play:

  • Arrow Keys or A/D - Move your spaceship left and right
  • Spacebar - Shoot bullets at enemies
  • Destroy all enemies to complete each level
  • Don't let enemies reach the bottom or you lose!
  • You start with 3 lives - avoid enemy fire!
  • Press R - Restart game anytime

Enemy Types:

  • 🔴 Red Enemies: Basic aliens, worth 100 points each
  • 🟠 Boss Enemies: Appear in Level 3+, worth 500 points each, stronger

Game Features:

  • 5 Levels: Each level has more enemies and they move faster
  • Lives System: Start with 3 lives, lose one when hit by enemy fire
  • Score Tracking: Earn points by destroying enemies
  • Enemy AI: Enemies randomly shoot back at you
  • Boss Battles: Special powerful enemies appear at higher levels

Tips:

  • Keep moving to avoid enemy bullets
  • Try to destroy enemies quickly before they descend
  • Boss enemies are worth more points but harder to defeat
  • You can shoot continuously - spam spacebar!
